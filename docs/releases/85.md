## Scout report reading

Release 84's native 360-by-640 inspection found 868.5 pixels of scout content constrained to a 55.6-pixel-high, 149-pixel-wide orders viewport. Source tests had not established adequate browser geometry. Release 85 makes the scout span the full active-orders grid. On narrow portrait and short landscape screens, an open report temporarily replaces the room and departure stack with the orders reading area. Other preparation stations remain available.

The existing disclosure summary becomes a sticky, minimum-44-pixel Close scout report / Back to preparation control. Opening brings the report header into its scroll owner; closing restores the prior orders scroll and summary focus. Selected threats, exact owned/reserve/unowned card inspection, cart cancellation, contract-trial restoration and stale-battle invalidation keep their established behavior. Inspection never purchases, equips, arms placement, changes progression or starts a field.

All 40 engine modules, live-HUD geometry, viewport diagnostics, economy, card stats, save formats and feature-off account state remain byte-identical to the prior release. No backend or provider activation is included.
