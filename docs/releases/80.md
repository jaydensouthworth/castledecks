## Optional decision-first comparison

Choosing an equipped destination inside the owned-card Inspector now places the replacement or swap consequence first, followed by equipped army-job changes and both cards' current ranks and actual per-squad gold/reserve costs. Auto-on, manual-only and reserve warnings remain adjacent to those costs. Bow abilities show that they have no squad deployment cost; unverified cards do not inherit invented costs or jobs from copied artwork. Swapping equipped cards still changes no deck membership or army-job coverage.

The source portrait remains visible at a compact size. Full card stats, descriptive notes, tactical caveats and current-rank numeric comparisons remain available behind native disclosures. Metric comparisons retain their measure, unit and scope boundaries; they are not total-damage or strength scores. Ordinary single-card inspection keeps its full expanded facts and existing placement label. Choosing No comparison restores that ordinary view.

Harmless render updates preserve disclosure elements, open state and focused summary identity. Entering a fresh comparison starts its details collapsed. Escape returns to the originating inspection control, retaining the prior armed card, search and pane positions. A stale review returns to full inspection and cannot arm a destination without a fresh choice. Comparison itself spends no resources and moves no cards. The existing Select action only arms and focuses a destination; actual placement still requires the existing action-bar commit.
