## Causeway Hold

The optional supplied practice field counts living eligible ground occupants inside its marked band. Earn 30 cumulative simulation seconds with at least one friendly occupant, no hostile occupant and a safe home flag. Contesting the band, leaving it or needing to recover the home flag pauses progress without erasing it. Occupation resolves after the full ordinary simulation tick. Securing the ground withdraws undispatched reserves; the actual finite enemy company must still be cleared and the home flag safe before victory. Hero, keep and flag losses retain their real defeat priority.

Normal paid troops can earn occupation while the hero stays garrisoned. Airborne, vehicle, sheltered, hidden, dead, foreign-world and otherwise ineligible actors do not gain occupation credit. Causeway-specific ground orders support the real objective without changing default encounters. Practice uses disposable supplies, gives no campaign reward and requires no purchased castle. Preparation explicitly reminds players that Basic Arrows can hit friendly ground troops.

Stranded-flag guidance and terminal detection consider actual owned contracts, living recoverers, pending arrivals, refundable reserves and known producers. Current gold, cooldowns, assignment or occupied battlefield slots alone do not prove recovery impossible. Unknown actors, producers or pending effects remain conservatively unresolved; no detector edits resources or queues.

## Castle keyboard continuity

Equip retains focus on the same castle choice using preventScroll, stable accessible names and aria-pressed. The prepared or eligible settled current choice remains focusable. Repeated Enter/Space, reselecting the current castle and stale reactivation have no effects: no rerender, refocus, announcement, cost or checkpoint write. Unowned, live-paused, read-only and stale-profile actions stay guarded. Keep both scroll panes, prepared keep identity, ability keys and the accepted portrait correction intact.
