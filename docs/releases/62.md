# Release 62 candidate

This source-tested candidate adds independent ground-company orders. Native acceptance of the new controls and feedback is pending. [Release 61](https://github.com/jaydensouthworth/castledecks/commit/ea29f0cb32631b3f561af1b4070bd7193ff61386) is the known-good rollback.

## Changes

- Choose All ground, Frontline or Support in Army, then Advance or a fixed Rear, Center or Forward line. Frontline and Support can hold separate lines or advance independently.
- Keep Support archers and priests behind their selected line. They regroup before starting new shots or healing, and use actual range rather than chasing while held.
- Finish committed actions normally and verify living recipients and actual range when held healing/purge releases. Switching to Advance mid-cast cannot bypass that check.
- Show split orders in the existing live controls. The live shortcut and live line selector explicitly replace both company orders with an All ground command.
- Let new troops inherit their role. Flag carriers and home-flag recovery take priority; flyers, siege, garrisons and Gorath keep their existing behavior. Enemy-keep destruction releases both ground companies.
- Explain actual Battery defeat causes in the result body and Pause brief. Home-flag capture and hero loss are reported from real state; forced or unknown defeat does not invent a cause.

All ground Rally now truly holds Support as well as Frontline. This intentional tactical change can make a badly positioned company less effective; it is not claimed to improve every outcome. Orders remain battle-only state and do not change contracts, save formats, costs, stats or earned campaign resources.

## Verification and accepted baseline

The exact candidate passed 1,641 combined engine, UI and recruitment checks with zero failures or skips. The public mirror preserves all 158 runtime files byte-for-byte and adds eight portable server/packaging checks, producing a full gate of 1,649 checks.

Coverage includes independent group state, role inheritance, real-range support movement and release validation, committed actions, live All ground overrides, invalid and stale requests, flag priorities, post-keep cleanup, and Battery defeat reasons. Thirty paired seeded default/explicit-Advance simulations retained deterministic parity with the preceding engine. Controlled actual-engine checks demonstrate that distant held support can lose healing and that releasing it allows ordinary movement back into real healing range. This is separate from native gameplay acceptance and does not establish human win rates.

The preceding release 61 is native-accepted: both Battery bodies were clear in short landscape with simulated safe insets; the formerly occluded rear engine was destroyed through ordinary firing; movement/Auto/precise controls remained usable; compact and portrait Army copy was readable; and resolving both engines correctly still lost after the home flag was captured. The earlier native Battery victory and fresh retry remain verified.

GitHub CI repeats source validation and the complete portable suite from pinned dependencies, then builds the actual Docker/Nginx image and checks configuration syntax, real routes, exact served bytes, module MIME, cache headers and missing-file responses.

Native acceptance of release 62 still needs company selection/pressed states, split markers/status, pause/reopen behavior, live All ground overrides, support movement/healing/purge and post-keep cleanup across portrait and short landscape. Automated DOM/Canvas tests do not establish these browser behaviors.

Physical Android/touch, orientation/lifecycle behavior, extended browser stability, native portable save-file round trips, broad balance and exact original Flash runtime equivalence remain unverified. Existing HUD coverage warnings and browser-local save restrictions remain. Export saves before changing website address or clearing site data.
