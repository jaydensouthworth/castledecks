## Compare before placing

The owned-card Inspector now includes an optional disclosure for comparing the inspected card with another occupied ability key. The preview distinguishes replacing an equipped card with a reserve card from swapping two already-equipped cards. A replaced card stays owned in reserve. A swap preserves deck membership and equipped army-job coverage. Comparisons are bounded by the current 30-key deck rather than the whole collection.

For verified cards, the preview explains gained or lost army jobs and when another equipped provider retains coverage. It uses each card's current owned rank and the active profile difficulty. Numeric differences require the same measure, unit and scope; missing data is shown as unverified rather than zero. Unknown or copied artwork identities acquire no invented jobs or statistics. These facts are not a total strength or damage score.

The preview distinguishes equipped capability from deployed troops. Rearranging spends no gold or reserve. Deployment notes explain each real squad's costs and whether Auto can spend repeatedly after Start or Resume. Sending a card to reserve stops its future reload/Auto recruitment without removing troops already on the field.

## Read-only preview and ordinary placement

Opening, choosing and cancelling a comparison do not move cards, purchase anything, advance simulation, or alter gold, reserve, timers or saves. Selection closes the Inspector, arms the inspected card and focuses its reviewed key on the appropriate bar. The ordinary next tap or Enter performs placement. Existing drag, engine and save-format code is unchanged.

Dismissal preserves the prior armed card, search, bar and scroll state. A reopened Inspector starts with no stale comparison. If the source, destination, ownership, profile or layout changes, selection blocks safely and asks for a fresh choice; a clearable No comparison option keeps ordinary placement available. Dismissed or old-profile callbacks cannot replace current intent. Nested comparison disclosures remain reachable within the Inspector's existing keyboard focus trap.
