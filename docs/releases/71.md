# Release 71 candidate

This candidate adds a meaningful second castle type, a dedicated deck slot and free player heraldry. Native acceptance is pending. [Accepted release 70](https://github.com/jaydensouthworth/castledecks/commit/1b2f2e40c0675c76f7d29f223ab57c353ce6283c) remains the rollback baseline. The source revision is `4f34b7493c5fddc8aaf9d88e77bd30b34f29fa9c`.

## Castle cards and decks

Classic Keep is the free default. Highwatch costs 1,500 in-game gold and purchasing it does not equip it. Highwatch raises the real firing station by 50 world units, has 80% of baseline keep health and two total shelter berths instead of four. Its taller masonry participates in ordinary collision. Resistance, recruitment prices/caps and the 30 ability keys are unchanged. Price and broad balance remain provisional; playable castle levels are not included.

The dedicated castle slot is inside Build's existing separate-slot disclosure and is preserved by saved decks. Before Start, equipping a castle changes the same pristine keep atomically without creating or rerolling the field. Mid-field swaps remain blocked even while paused; normal same-castle ability edits remain available. Settled castle choices apply to the next field.

Three free profile-local color schemes expose Preview, Apply and Cancel. Allied keep, flag, hero and human cloth use their actual palette tokens plus fixed two-tone ally chevrons. Enemy, elemental, status and health cues remain distinct. Importing a deck does not alter profile colors.

## Optional Highwatch practice

The SK2 Highwatch field supplies both castles only to its disposable practice profile. Enemy castles use the same mechanics. Existing SK1 descriptors, defaults and campaign stages remain unchanged; 144 pinned legacy starting-field fixtures are retained. The optional field requires enemy-keep destruction, finite cleanup and a safe home flag. Its opening enemy archers leave their gallery on tick 1 under unchanged AI, so this is not an active opening gallery defense.

The reproducible [engine comparison report](../../tests/castle-balance70-evidence.md) and [full receipts](../../tests/castle-balance70-evidence.json) distinguish ordinary paid schedules from explicitly authored terrain/gallery fixtures. They demonstrate trajectory, collision, health and shelter tradeoffs. Three practice threat levels and deterministic outcomes do not establish broad human difficulty or campaign balance.

## Save compatibility

Explicit profile-3, bundle-2, deck-2, profile-decks-2, local-checkpoint-3 and charter-2 schemas retain old imports as Classic. Unknown future schemas, castle IDs, castle levels and palettes preserve both local banks. Independent old-44/69 reader fixtures are kept intact, pinned and tested to refuse newer data. No production downgrader is included. Export backups before changing website address or clearing browser data.
