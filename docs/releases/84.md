## Scout company

The active orders retain their short threat overview and add a closed-by-default Scout company disclosure. It lists every actual starting-roster type, explicitly including already deployed reserves rather than claiming remaining counts. Selecting a threat shows the atlas's shared counter advice and notes. A bounded suggested bow card is identified as equipped (real bar and key), owned reserve, or unowned. Review opens that exact card in Build without arming a placement, or in Armory. Inspection does not purchase, equip, start a field, change rank or alter the economy. Cavalry receives strategy advice without an invented single-card recommendation; unknown enemy IDs receive explicit unavailable advice. Aliases use the existing canonical intel identity. Settled skirmish and completed-campaign review actions are disabled with an explicit new-field note.

Inspection context belongs to the actual battle identity. The selected threat survives a disposable contract-trial detour while a new field invalidates old company information. Existing panel-close routing restores focus to the scout review control without changing a browsed destination. Existing disclosure and modal semantics remain in use. Scout controls are at least 44px, normal copy is 14px, and secondary basis notes are 12px. The disclosure is inside the existing orders scroll, not the live HUD.

All 40 engine modules, compact live-HUD geometry, viewport diagnostics, command semantics, save formats and feature-off account state are unchanged from accepted 83. This release adds no backend or provider activation.
