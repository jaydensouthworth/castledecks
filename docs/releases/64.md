This source-tested candidate adds disposable live Armory contract trials. Native acceptance of their launch, return, feedback and responsive layouts is pending. [Release 63](https://github.com/jaydensouthworth/castledecks/commit/046526942a8b8e15e8d9d3618c166ec1a6a660f2) is the known-good rollback.

## Changes

- Try any of the 12 regular Army contract cards, including an unowned contract, from the live Armory.
- Use a clearly assisted disposable profile with 500 trial gold, Basic Arrow and the selected contract. Owned contract rank, hero rank and difficulty are copied by value; unowned contracts begin at rank zero.
- Pay ordinary recruitment costs and use real reserve, cooldown, arrival, AI, attacks, healing and damage. Troops still take normal damage. The protected hero, stopped reinforcements and disabled settlement are explicit practice fixtures; no campaign unlock or progress is earned.
- Give Priest a real wounded friendly Archer and ordinary contracts two enemy Grunts. Trebuchet keeps an ordinary distant hostile keep and displays its actual loading/aiming phase instead of skipping its windup.
- Return to the original card, cart, wishlist, query/scroll and paused management state, restoring the original profile, battle, clock and motion state. Existing cached Training is preserved.
- Guard temporary-session saves/imports, including asynchronous file-read completion, while keeping the ordinary assisted Playground saveable.

Every engine file is byte-for-byte unchanged from accepted release 63. The trial layer prepares disposable fixtures and reuses ordinary gameplay rather than adding combat-stat shortcuts or permanent card ownership.
