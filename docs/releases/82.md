## Portrait controls

Portrait skill, page, companion, movement and contextual action targets are 48px tall. Widths retain their accepted minimum of 44px, and names and cooldowns remain present. Precision and airborne actions keep their full action and detail labels; their decorative pseudo-icons are omitted in portrait to avoid overfilling the shorter controls.

Movement and contextual actions sit 8px above the actual measured arsenal rather than using a fixed height assumption. This follows sparse and full action bars, company controls, companion utilities and font-driven caption growth. The presentation-only `syncPortraitDock` helper ignores landscape and square viewports, absent elements, and invalid or hidden geometry. It writes only when the measured dock height changes. The existing render pass invokes it before reading movement geometry.

Landscape placement is unchanged. All 40 engine modules, viewport diagnostics, ability statistics, resource rules, persistence and command semantics remain unchanged. No new gameplay feature, backend or provider activation is included. Existing portrait camera framing reads the movement controls' top edge and can use the released space; no direct camera code changes. That rendering consequence remains part of native acceptance.
